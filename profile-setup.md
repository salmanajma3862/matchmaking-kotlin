# Profile Setup Screens Plan

## Total Screens: 8

### Screen Sequence & Logic

**1. Basic Info Screen**
- Fields: Name, Gender, Date of Birth
- Why first: Essential identity data needed before anything else

**2. Location Screen**
- Fields: City, Country, Address (optional)
- Why second: Required for matchmaking, better to collect early

**3. Photos Upload Screen**
- Fields: Profile photos (min 1, max 6)
- Why third: Visual identity is crucial, keeps users engaged early

**4. Physical & Personal Details Screen**
- Fields: Height, Weight, Body Type, Bio
- Why fourth: Completes basic profile appearance

**5. Background & Lifestyle Screen**
- Fields: Religion, Sect, Marital Status, Education, Profession, Income Range
- Why fifth: Core matchmaking criteria

**6. Habits & Preferences Screen**
- Fields: Smoking, Drinking, Diet Preference, Living with Family
- Why sixth: Lifestyle compatibility factors

**7. Interests & Hobbies Screen**
- Fields: Interests (multi-select), Hobbies (multi-select)
- Why seventh: Personality and connection building

**8. Marriage Intentions & Preferences Screen**
- Fields: 
  - Intention (marriage/friendship/dating)
  - Ready for marriage timeframe
  - Partner preferences (age range, city, gender, marital status, religion, education, search radius)
- Why last: Users understand their own profile first, then define what they're looking for

**9. Verification Selfie Screen (Optional)**
- Fields: Selfie upload for verification
- Why optional last: Not mandatory, can be skipped, done after profile completion

## Key Points

- **Progressive disclosure**: Start simple, get more detailed
- **Engagement first**: Photos early to keep users interested
- **Logic flow**: Self → Lifestyle → Preferences
- **Validation**: Each screen validates before moving forward
- **Skip options**: Allow skip on optional fields (bio, hobbies, verification)
- **Progress indicator**: Show 1/8, 2/8, etc.
- **Save draft**: Auto-save progress at each step
